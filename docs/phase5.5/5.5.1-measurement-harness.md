# 5.5.1 — The `construction-study` harness

*The instrument. Nothing about shorting is built here — this measures whether it would be worth building.*

## Read first

MASTER §2 row **D123** (and **D122**, which makes the expectation a measured property of the construction);
§20.3 (the detectability gate and its floor); **D91** / §24.5 (the Signal-Library descriptive-only rail);
D117 clause 1 (report-only verbs); rule 4 (point-in-time), rule 5 (costs always on), rule 16 (D52
pre-registration). Code: `DetectabilityGate.cs`, `MdeCalculator.cs`, `BarFeatureView.cs`,
`SignalRegistry.cs`, `StoreSweepOrchestrator.cs` (the report-only verb precedent).

## Build

1. **`src/AlphaLab.Evaluation/Construction/ConstructionStudy.cs`** — the result types and
   `ConstructionStudyOptions`. Options are **CLI flags with defaults, never CONFIG keys**: they are study
   parameters recorded in the artefact, not lab settings, and inventing `ConstructionStudy:*` keys for a
   report-only verb would break the "never invent a key" rule. Horizon, confidence and power come from
   the existing `Gate` section so the floor is the *same arithmetic the admission gate applies*.
2. **`ConstructionStudyEngine.cs`** — `AdjClosePanel` plus the one-pass walk. Per signal, monthly
   rebalance (first session of each calendar month), top tail long, bottom tail short.
3. **`src/AlphaLab.Worker/Ops/ConstructionStudyOrchestrator.cs`** — panel load, wiring, the rendered
   artefact under `docs/calibration/{arena}/{date}-construction-study.md`.
4. **The verb** — `construction-study --from --to [--arena] [--tail-fraction] [--borrow-bp ...]`, on the
   `store-sweep` model. `--borrow-bp` is repeatable; each value is one stated assumption.

## Rails this checkpoint must not cross

- **No second point-in-time view.** The scoring path uses the real `BarFeatureView`. Its own docstring is
  the argument: *"there is deliberately no second place for the watermark rule to drift."* The
  realisation panel is a different thing — it resolves the return a position **already held** earned
  between two past sessions, which is backward-looking by definition (the `BandInputs` precedent,
  §25.2). It loads through `IBarReadService.GetCrossSection`, so even the panel does not restate the
  watermark rule.
- **Report-only.** No `SoleWriterGate`, no transaction, no row, no flag, no config value.
- **`Construction/` is not a consumer directory.** `ci.ps1` scans `Allocator`, `Gate`, `Candidates`,
  `Power` and `Core/Funnel` for `ISignal` mentions. This namespace is deliberately outside that set and
  **must never be added to it** — that is the structural form of D91's descriptive-only rule.
- **The benchmark is the SCORED set**, not the eligible pool (finding 294). Otherwise "thin-history names
  behaved differently" is folded into what the report calls the signal's active return.

## Traps already known — do not rediscover these

**1. The geometric-ramp fixture that measures nothing.** The first all-seven fixture built its panel from
pure geometric ramps and passed. It was hollow: on a ramp every daily return is identical, so market
return variance is ~1e-32 — floating-point residue. `bab` and `resmom` divided by it and got noise
instead of a beta; `lowvol` saw zero volatility everywhere; `brk` scored 1.0 for every name. **Four of
seven signals were degenerate and the assertions — finite, non-negative, non-empty — were all satisfied.**
Use a one-factor panel with real beta and idiosyncratic-vol dispersion, and assert TE is *strictly*
positive plus that the seven produce distinguishable results.

**2. The per-signal pass that cannot finish.** Measuring one signal at a time rebuilds `BarFeatureView` at
every rebalance for every signal, and none of them share the window memoization the view exists to
provide. Over 24 years that is ~5 million windowed bar queries instead of ~720 thousand. Walk the
sessions **once**, build one view per rebalance date, let all seven scorers use it, and drop it before
the next. `TurnoverCost` takes the *same* view rather than resolving its own.

**3. The double-charged long leg.** The long leg's cost belongs to both constructions, so compute it once
and add it twice. Calling the priced walk twice also double-counts `Uncosted`, quietly halving the
reader's only check on the cost figure.

**4. Ties are ordinary.** `brk:L252` saturates at 1.0 for every name at its high. Without a
`security_id` tie-break the tail cut is not reproducible.

**5. Cost is a drag on the MEAN, never folded into the series TE is measured from.** A monthly cost lands
as a lump on twelve days a year; charging it to the series adds variance that is an artefact of the
rebalance calendar, not a property of the construction — and TE is the number the study turns on.
