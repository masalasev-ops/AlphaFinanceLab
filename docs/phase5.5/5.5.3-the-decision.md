# 5.5.3 — The decision

*The checkpoint that makes this a phase rather than a spike: it ends.*

## Read first

The archived report from 5.5.2; MASTER §2 **D123**; **D122** (the expectation is measured, not asserted);
**D121** (the ten-year horizon and why it was pre-registered before the curves existed); D116 (the
ceiling); §1.2 (the laboratory KPIs the lab is actually graded on).

## The rule, written before the numbers are read

| result | conclusion |
|---|---|
| IR gain **materially > 1** *and* years-to-detect falls inside the gate horizon | **Long-short is justified.** Proceed to the build phase. |
| IR gain **≈ 1.0×** | The construction bought **leverage, not information**. Long-only stands. **Weeks saved.** |
| IR gain > 1 but years-to-detect still beyond the horizon | Real but insufficient. Recorded as such; the horizon is a *separate* decision and is not reopened here to rescue this one. |
| The verdict **flips between borrow assumptions** | The answer depends on data this arena does not have. **Decide nothing**; acquiring borrow data becomes the next question. |

**Decided on the rule text and the measurement — never on which answer is more convenient.** The banding
in the report is a reading aid; no verdict, gate or config consumes it.

## What a "no" costs and what it is worth

A "no" is the cheap outcome and a publishable one. §1.2 grades this lab on operator learning and verdict
honesty, not on having found a winner — *"this arena cannot adjudicate a realistic edge under either
construction"* is a real result about the instrument, and it arrives for the price of one report instead
of a phase of ledger work, a borrow-cost model, corporate-action inversion and a fresh calibration
generation.

## The rail that outlives the decision

Whatever is decided, **the study's numbers may never set a pre-registered `expected_effect_ann`**
(rule 16 / D52). Choosing the number you then pre-register by looking at measured data is precisely what
pre-registration exists to prevent. This decides *which construction the lab builds*, never *what a
strategy claims*. The rendered report repeats this where a future reader will meet it, which is the only
place a rail like this survives.

## If the answer is yes — what is NOT in this phase

Named here so the boundary is explicit and the phase cannot sprawl into the thing it was measuring:

- `TradeSide.Short` and the ledger changes it implies
- a borrow-cost term in D43 (and the cost-model version bump that forces)
- corporate-action handling inverted for short positions
- margin and financing — **D84 forbids implicit leverage and is not reopened here**
- new control populations matched to a long-short construction
- a new calibration generation, and the frozen curves it would replace

Each of those is phase-sized. They belong to a decision made *after* this one, under its own number.

## DoD

The decision is recorded in PROGRESS and CHANGELOG **whichever way it goes**, citing the report by path
and quoting the IR gain and years-to-detect it rests on. If the answer is yes, the follow-on phase is
scoped but **not started**.
