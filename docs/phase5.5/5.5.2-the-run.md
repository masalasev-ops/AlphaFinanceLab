## THE TRAP AT THE CENTRE OF THIS PHASE

**Do not compare the two constructions' detectability floors.** This is not a caution about a mistake
someone might make; it is a correction the study forced on its own first design.

A long-short book is roughly **2× leverage on the same cross-sectional bet**. It scales tracking error
*and* effect together, so the floor rises exactly as fast as the effect that has to clear it. Since the
t-statistic is `IR·√T`, detectability depends on the **information ratio alone**, and a construction that
doubles both terms changes nothing.

The 2020–21 smoke run said so outright:

| signal | TE ratio | effect ratio | IR long-only | IR long-short |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `resmom:L252` | ×3.01 | ×3.01 | 0.374 | 0.373 |

Long-short bought **leverage and nothing else** for that signal. `FX-ConstructionScaleFree` now pins the
arithmetic so the framing cannot regress.

**Read instead:** the information-ratio gain, and `years-to-detect = (ZSum/IR)²`, which *is* comparable
across constructions.

## The artefact

- Lands at `docs/calibration/{arena}/{date}-construction-study.md`.
- **Commit it in the same PR.** Findings 362/363 are the reason: a report that is regenerated before it
  is committed stops being evidence, and `Calibration.ReportRef` went two whole generations unverified.
  `docs/calibration/** -text` already pins the line endings so a delete-and-checkout round-trip is exact.
- **Never regenerate it to "clean it up".** If it is wrong, the next run gets its own dated file and the
  wrong one stays, with a note. An archive that can be rewritten is not an archive.

## What the reader must carry out of it

- TE is **NW-corrected** (σ_LR, not naive) — so these floors are *larger* than a naive TE implies. A study
  arguing for a lower floor must not pick the estimator that flatters it.
- The floor here carries **no Bonferroni trials haircut**, deliberately: that haircut is a property of how
  many candidates the arena has registered, and including it would make the two constructions differ by
  the trials count as well as by their tracking error.
- **Borrow is an assumption.** The 0 bp column is the optimistic bound. If the conclusion is the same at
  both assumptions, the missing borrow data does not matter here — and the report says so explicitly. If
  it flips, *that* is the finding, and the decision waits for real borrow data.
- **A signal reporting zero rebalances is a data gap, not a result** — most likely a missing market proxy,
  which leaves `resmom:L252` and `bab:L252` scoring nothing at all.
- The D49 survivorship caveat and the D120 exclusions both still ride on every number here.
