## What landed

### D112 — the researcher's evidence diet (closes P8)

The hypotheses endpoint refuses (422, named reason) once the **count of overdue journal outcomes reaches
`Research.MaxConcurrentCandidates`** (3).

**The grace window's SHAPE, not its constant.** A `Research.OutcomeOverdueGraceDays` key would have been an
undefended number of exactly the class finding 309 had just flagged in `Research.ForkBudgetPerYear`. The
bound is instead *derived*: CONFIG_REFERENCE ties `MaxConcurrentCandidates` to §8's *"1 Live + 2–3
Candidates … bounded by statistical honesty, not compute"*, making it the count of claims the lab can
honestly hold in flight — the right quantity to measure a saturated evidence base against.

Three other existing numbers were checked and rejected on the record: `ForkBudgetPerYear` (itself
underived), `Kpi.CohortMinStrategies` (numerically 3, but a cohort *rendering* threshold), and
`evidence_window_days` (it *defines* "overdue", so it cannot also bound it).

Two clauses are in the decision because the mechanism is misreadable without them: the trigger is
**operator** behaviour, not researcher behaviour; and **every refusal is counted and published beside the
D110 score**, because an unattributed gap in the proposal stream reads as researcher inactivity when it is
operator debt.

### D113 — the control arm is a PAPER control, differenced on the evidence-prior seam

D110 priced this at a doubled trials tax. **The premise was withdrawn, verified in code rather than
argued:** `DetectabilityGate.Assess` is a pure read returning `FloorAnn` on both paths, and the only
forward `TrialsRegistry.Add` is inside `CandidateFactory.Create`. `score_detect` is computed at *proposal*
time; the tax is paid at *admission*.

**What it controls FOR is the load-bearing half.** "Never admitted, same run" describes a *duplicate* —
two identical researchers produce a margin difference of zero by construction. With no learning mechanism
to withhold before Phase 8, the arm difference is the **evidence-prior seam** §24.6 already requires to be
swappable/disableable/placebo-able. Treatment = digest wired; control = the same seat with the digest
**placebo'd** (default; identical shape and token count) or disabled.

Two pairing constraints are structural: **same run, before any admission** (the floor moves by a
Bonferroni step otherwise), and **shared budget must abstain both arms or neither**.

**D113 amends D110:** `detectability_floor_ann` is the floor as at **assessment**, not admission — a paper
control never reaches admission. D110's Status cell reads `amended-by D113`.
